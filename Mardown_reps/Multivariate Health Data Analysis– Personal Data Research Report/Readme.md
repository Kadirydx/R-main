# Socio-Economic and Health Data Analysis 

## About the Project
This research project investigates the complex relationships between sociodemographic factors, lifestyle habits, physical health, and mental well-being. By merging multiple raw datasets—including personal details, depression scores, sleep timing, and medical indexes—the study creates a unified framework for in-depth statistical analysis. The primary goal is to uncover how variables like education, occupation, smoking, and alcohol consumption impact depression and overall health scores.

##  Technologies Used
* **R & R Markdown:** Statistical computing, hypothesis testing, and dynamic document generation.
* **dplyr & tidyr:** Comprehensive data wrangling, cleaning, and merging of disparate datasets.
* **ggplot2, ggcorrplot & corrplot:** Advanced data visualization and correlation matrix plotting.
* **hrbrthemes & viridis:** Enhancing the visual aesthetics and readability of the generated plots.

##  Key Features
* **Extensive Data Wrangling:** Successfully combining five different raw datasets and thoroughly restructuring variables such as age, sex, social class, and lifestyle habits.
* **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Visualizing distributions, identifying missing values, and generating correlation matrices to understand the relationships between health metrics.
* **Statistical Hypothesis Testing:** Applying Chi-Square Tests, ANOVA, and Tukey's HSD to determine the statistical significance between variables like smoking/drinking habits and DLact health scores.
* **Mental Health Insights:** Investigating the direct impacts of education level (ISCED 97) and employment status on emotional state and depression.
